My progress disappeared

It depends on whether you were signed in. Playing as a guest keeps your island on the device and nowhere else, so deleting the app deletes the save with it and there is no copy anywhere for me to restore. Signing in with Apple or Google copies your island to a server, and signing in again on a new phone brings it back.

If you were signed in and your island did not come back, email me from the address you signed in with and I will look for it.

I paid and did not get my coins or spins

The game keeps a private record of every purchase Apple or Google has confirmed, and re-checks it each time you launch. If a purchase was interrupted — the app closed mid-payment, or the network dropped — reopening the game is usually enough to deliver it.

If it still has not arrived after a relaunch, email me with the date and the pack you bought and I will look into it.

I want a refund

All purchases go through the store you installed the game from, and only that store can refund them — I never see your payment details and cannot issue one myself. On the App Store, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com or via Settings on your device. On Google Play, request one from your order history. The store decides each request.

How do free spins work?

You get 3 spins back every 2 minutes, up to a maximum of 50. Spins earned from rewards, quests and packs are added on top and are not limited by that cap.

What are the chances of getting a particular card?

Every chest shows its complete odds table inside the purchase screen, above the pay button, before you are charged. Nothing is hidden behind a tap.

Turning notifications off

In the game: Options → Notifications. Or system-wide, in iOS Settings under Notifications → Loot Lagoon, or in Android Settings under Apps → Loot Lagoon → Notifications.

Deleting your data

Options → Delete Account erases your island, your cards and all local copies of the save immediately. Deleting the app does the same thing. Neither can be undone.
