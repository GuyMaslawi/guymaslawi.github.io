The short version. You play Loot Lagoon signed in with Apple or Google. Your island is kept on a server so it survives losing or replacing your phone, and other players can see the name and face you choose. There is no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Who this is about

Loot Lagoon is published by GM-BUSINESS, a sole proprietorship owned and operated by Guy Maslawi in Israel. Questions about this policy or about your data go to guymuslave@gmail.com.

Why the game needs an account

Raids land on other players’ islands, clans and the league need a name to answer to, and what you buy lives in your island. An account is what keeps all of that yours, and what brings it back when you change or lose your phone. Earlier versions let you play as a guest, with the island kept only on the device; that island is carried into your account the first time you sign in.

What is collected

Signing in with Apple or Google creates an account and keeps your island on a server, so that deleting the app or changing phone does not lose it. The following is collected:

  • An account identifier from Apple or Google, and the identifier the game assigns you. These are what connect you to your island.
  • An email address, passed on by Apple or Google when you sign in. If you used Sign in with Apple and chose Hide My Email, what I receive is Apple’s relay address rather than your real one. The email is never shown to other players and is never used to match accounts.
  • The name and face you choose, which other players can see. The name is first suggested from your Apple or Google profile, and you can change it at any time in Options.
  • Your game state — coins, spins, island level, buildings, cards, collections, quest progress, notification preferences, and a record of which purchases have been delivered to you.
  • Raids — that a raid happened between two islands and what was taken, so the player who was raided can be shown it when they next open the game.
  • Reports and blocks you make, so the person reported stops being offered to you and so repeated reports can be acted on.
  • Crash and usage reports — the game’s build, the device model and system version, a random install identifier that reinstalling the app replaces, where the game was when it closed unexpectedly, and counts of what was used in a session (spins, screens opened). They contain no name, email or location, and are used only to find and fix problems.

What other players can see

Only the name and face you chose, your island number, your star count and how much is in your vault — the same things you can see about them. Your email address, your account identifiers and your purchase history are never shown to another player.

If a name is aimed at you or is offensive, the flag beside it on the leaderboard reports it and stops that player being matched with you again.

Anything you type in a clan chat is stored and shown to everyone in that clan, together with your name, your face and your star count. It is not shown to anybody outside the clan, it is never made public, and there is no direct messaging and no way to reach a player who is not a clanmate. Do not put anything in a clan chat that you would not want a stranger to read — the other members of a clan are usually strangers, and one of them may keep a screenshot. If you block or report somebody, their messages stop appearing for you and yours stop appearing for them, at once and in both directions.

Where it is stored

On servers operated by Supabase, in the European Union, acting as my data processor. Supabase also handles the sign-in itself. No other company receives any of it: there is no analytics provider, no advertising network and no data broker involved at any point.

Why it is collected

For the app to work, and for nothing else. The account exists so your island survives your phone; the game state is the island; the raid records are the game. None of it is used to profile you, to advertise to you, or to personalise what you are shown.

Tracking

Loot Lagoon does not track you. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, contains no analytics or advertising SDK, and shares nothing with anyone for advertising purposes. The app’s privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by the store you installed the game from — Apple on the App Store, Google on Google Play. I never receive, see or store your payment card, your billing address, or your Apple or Google account. What is recorded is only which purchases that store has confirmed, so that a pack interrupted mid-delivery can be handed over on the next launch, and so that what you paid for is restored with your island.

The store’s own handling of a purchase is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy or Google’s Privacy Policy, not this one.

How long it is kept

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account the island is marked deleted immediately and stops appearing anywhere in the game, and the sign-ins attached to it are removed at once. The island itself is erased after thirty days — that window exists so that a deletion made by mistake, or the wrong island chosen when two are merged, can still be undone if you write to me.

Clan chat messages are deleted automatically three days after they are sent, whether or not you still have an account, and asking a clan for spins or a card is a message like any other. A report is kept for longer, and deliberately: one report is an argument and twenty is a pattern, and I cannot see the pattern if each one is erased with the message that caused it. A report holds the message, who wrote it, who reported it and when — nothing else.

Children

Loot Lagoon contains simulated gambling and randomised paid items. It is rated 17+ on the App Store and 18+ on Google Play, is not directed to children, and is not part of any children’s category. I do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, write to me and I will delete it.

Your rights

Options → Delete account & data deletes your account from inside the app, without emailing anyone: it removes your island from the server and from this device, along with the sign-ins attached to it. Deleting the app alone removes the copy on your phone but not the one on the server, so use the in-app option if you want it all gone.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, I am the controller of the data described above and Supabase is the processor. The lawful basis is performance of the contract between us — providing the game you asked for. You have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or erase your data, and to complain to your local supervisory authority. Write to guymuslave@gmail.com and I will answer.
